How to play. Scoop clay from the pit, pour it into clay molds (1 kg per brick) and let it bake. Carry the bricks to the pyramid and place them for pay.

Bench press (Strength): hit Space in the green. Treadmill (Endurance): spam W to stay ahead of the red line. Brick Bazaar: click it or press E nearby.

Hammer (3): RMB opens blueprints · LMB places · R rotates · Hold E picks things up to move them · Q cancels.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · mouse look · click/F act · 1 2 3 hands, bowl, hammer · Q drop a brick (hold: drop all) · G give a brick to the teammate you face (hold: all) · M sound · Esc pause.
