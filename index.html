How to play. Scoop clay from the pit, pour it into clay molds (1 kg per brick) and let it bake. Carry the bricks to the pyramid and place them for pay.

Bench press (Strength): hit Space in the green. Treadmill (Endurance): spam W to stay ahead of the red line. Market stand: click it or press E nearby.

Hammer (3): E opens blueprints · LMB places · R rotates · RMB relocates · Q refunds while relocating.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · mouse look · click/F act · 1 2 3 hands, bowl, hammer · Q drop a brick (hold: drop all) · M sound · Esc pause.
